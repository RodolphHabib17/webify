## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This document identifies the primary user groups Webify aims to serve. It describes their goals, needs, frustrations, behaviors, and expectations when looking for website solutions.

These personas will guide Webify's UI/UX design decisions, feature prioritization, customer journey, and overall product development.

### 1.2 Product Overview

Webify is a website-template marketplace that allows customers to:

- Browse and explore professionally designed website templates.
- Preview templates before making a purchase.
- Select a template that matches their needs.
- Customize approved website elements through a controlled visual editor.
- Submit orders and track their progress.
- Receive a completed website through the platform.
- Request additional custom website development services when needed.

Webify will initially target customers in Lebanon, with the possibility of expanding into international markets in the future.

### 1.3 Persona Research Status

The personas in this document are initial assumptions based on Webify's proposed business model and target market. They are not based on completed customer interviews or validated market research.

They should be reviewed and updated after collecting feedback from potential customers.

## 2. Primary Customer Personas

### Persona 1 — Small Business Owner

**Name:** Maya  
**Age:** 34  
**Occupation:** Owner of a small retail business  
**Location:** Lebanon  
**Technical Experience:** Basic

#### Background

Maya owns a small business and wants to establish a professional online presence. Most of her customers currently discover her business through social media and personal recommendations.

She wants a website where potential customers can learn about her products, services, location, and contact information.

However, hiring a web development agency may be too expensive or complicated for her current business needs.

#### Goals

- Establish a professional online presence.
- Present products and services clearly.
- Make it easier for customers to contact her.
- Find an affordable website solution.
- Update basic website information without technical assistance.

#### Needs

- Affordable and professionally designed templates.
- Simple navigation and clear instructions.
- Mobile-responsive website designs.
- Easy editing of text, images, colors, and contact information.
- Clear pricing and ordering information.
- Reliable customer support.

#### Frustrations

- High website development costs.
- Difficulty understanding technical terminology.
- Complicated website-building tools.
- Uncertainty about what is included in a website package.
- Difficulty finding affordable developers.

#### Expected Behavior

1. Visits the Webify homepage.
2. Explores available website templates.
3. Filters templates according to her business category.
4. Opens a template to view its details and preview.
5. Selects a suitable template.
6. Registers and submits an order.
7. Customizes basic website information.
8. Reviews the website before delivery.

#### How Webify Helps

Webify provides ready-made business templates and a guided customization experience, helping small business owners establish an online presence without needing advanced technical knowledge.

## 3. Internal User Persona

### Persona 5 — Webify Administrator

**Name:** Webify Admin  
**Age:** Not specified  
**Occupation:** Platform Administrator  
**Technical Experience:** Advanced

#### Background

The administrator is responsible for managing Webify's marketplace and ensuring that the platform operates correctly.

The administrator manages templates, customer accounts, orders, payment records, and website delivery.

#### Goals

- Maintain an organized template marketplace.
- Manage customer accounts and orders.
- Monitor payment statuses.
- Ensure that purchased websites are delivered correctly.
- Maintain platform security and reliability.
- Resolve customer issues.

#### Needs

- Secure administrative dashboard.
- Template management tools.
- Customer and order management.
- Payment status management.
- Website delivery and publishing controls.
- Access control and activity tracking.

#### Frustrations

- Incomplete customer information.
- Incorrect or duplicated orders.
- Payment verification difficulties.
- Disorganized template management.
- Security and operational issues.

#### Expected Behavior

1. Logs into the administrative dashboard.
2. Reviews new orders.
3. Verifies payment information.
4. Manages customer requests.
5. Adds, updates, or removes templates.
6. Monitors website delivery.
7. Handles operational issues.

#### How Webify Helps

Webify provides centralized administrative tools to manage marketplace operations, customer activity, templates, and order workflows.

## 5. Common User Needs

Across the primary customer personas, Webify should prioritize:

1. **Affordability:** Clear pricing and accessible website solutions.
2. **Simplicity:** An understandable customer journey with minimal technical complexity.
3. **Professional Design:** Modern, attractive, and responsive templates.
4. **Customization:** The ability to personalize approved website content.
5. **Transparency:** Clear information about orders, payments, services, and delivery.
6. **Reliability:** A secure and dependable platform.
7. **Support:** Assistance when customers encounter problems.
8. **Accessibility:** Designs that work across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 6. Design Implications

The identified personas should influence the following UI/UX decisions:

| Area | Design Requirement |
|---|---|
| Homepage | Clearly explain Webify's value and guide visitors toward templates |
| Marketplace | Provide understandable categories and search functionality |
| Template Details | Display previews, descriptions, features, and pricing |
| Registration | Keep account creation straightforward |
| Customer Dashboard | Make orders, projects, and account information easy to access |
| Customization Editor | Use clear controls and avoid unnecessary technical complexity |
| Order Management | Clearly communicate order and payment statuses |
| Customer Support | Make assistance and contact information accessible |
| Responsive Design | Ensure a consistent experience across devices |
| Localization | Plan for Arabic, English, and RTL support |

## 7. Assumptions and Future Research

The following assumptions require validation:

- Small businesses in Lebanon are interested in affordable ready-made websites.
- Freelancers value portfolio templates and easy customization.
- Startup founders may prefer templates before investing in custom development.
- Organizations and content creators need simple content-focused websites.
- Customers are comfortable submitting orders and payment information through an online platform.
- Arabic and English support may improve accessibility for the initial market.

### Recommended Validation Activities

Before finalizing these personas:

1. Interview at least 2–3 potential customers from each primary customer group where possible.
2. Ask how they currently establish and maintain their online presence.
3. Identify their biggest difficulties when purchasing or developing a website.
4. Understand their preferred website features and customization needs.
5. Ask what would make them trust an online website-template marketplace.
6. Review and update personas using actual feedback.

---

## 8. Conclusion

Webify should initially focus on small business owners, freelancers, startup founders, and content creators or organizations that need accessible and professional website solutions.

The administrator persona represents the internal operational requirements of the platform.

These personas provide a foundation for the next UI/UX activities, including the website sitemap, customer user flows, administrator user flows, and design system.
