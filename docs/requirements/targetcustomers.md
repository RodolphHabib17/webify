\## 1. Introduction



This document identifies the primary and secondary customer segments that Webify aims to serve.



Webify is a website-template marketplace and visual website-building platform that helps individuals and businesses create, customize, and publish professional websites without requiring advanced programming knowledge.



The platform will initially focus on customers in Lebanon, with the objective of expanding to international markets in the future.



Our target customers include:



1\. Small and medium-sized businesses.

2\. Freelancers and independent professionals.

3\. Entrepreneurs and startup founders.

4\. Digital agencies and web development companies.



\---



\## 2. Primary Target Customers



\### 2.1 Small and Medium-Sized Businesses (SMBs)



\*\*Description:\*\*



Small businesses that need a professional online presence to promote their products, services, and brand.



\*\*Examples:\*\*

\- Restaurants and cafés.

\- Clothing and retail stores.

\- Beauty salons and barbershops.

\- Fitness centers and gyms.

\- Real estate businesses.

\- Consulting companies.

\- Local service providers.



\*\*Main Needs:\*\*

\- Affordable professional websites.

\- Business information and contact pages.

\- Product and service showcases.

\- Mobile-friendly designs.

\- Contact forms and social media integration.

\- Simple website customization.



\*\*Main Challenges:\*\*

\- High costs of traditional website development.

\- Limited technical knowledge.

\- Difficulty finding reliable developers.

\- Lack of time to manage website development.



\*\*How Webify Helps:\*\*



Webify provides ready-made business templates that customers can customize using a visual editor and publish without building everything from scratch.



\*\*Customer Priority:\*\* High



\---



\### 2.2 Freelancers and Independent Professionals

\## 5. Customer Pain Points



Webify aims to address the following common customer problems:



1\. \*\*High development costs:\*\* Traditional website development may be too expensive for small businesses and individuals.

2\. \*\*Limited technical knowledge:\*\* Many potential customers cannot build websites independently.

3\. \*\*Time-consuming development:\*\* Building a website from scratch can take significant time.

4\. \*\*Limited customization:\*\* Some ready-made website solutions do not offer the flexibility customers need.

5\. \*\*Finding reliable developers:\*\* Businesses may struggle to find affordable and trustworthy development services.

6\. \*\*Lack of professional presentation:\*\* Individuals and businesses need modern websites to establish credibility online.

\## 7. Initial Market Focus



\### Geographic Focus



\*\*Initial Market:\*\* Lebanon



The first stage will focus on understanding the needs of Lebanese small businesses, freelancers, and entrepreneurs.



\*\*Future Markets:\*\* International expansion will be considered after validating the product and establishing a sustainable business model.



\### Initial Customer Priorities



The recommended initial customer acquisition order is:



1\. Small businesses that need simple professional websites.

2\. Freelancers who need portfolio and personal websites.

3\. Entrepreneurs who need startup and landing-page websites.

4\. Digital agencies that may benefit from reusable templates and advanced workflows.



This order is a starting hypothesis and should be validated through customer interviews and market research.



\---



\## 8. Customer Validation Strategy



Before investing heavily in advanced features, Webify should validate the needs of its target customers.



\### Research Activities



\- Interview local small business owners.

\- Speak with freelancers and independent professionals.

\- Interview startup founders.

\- Contact digital agencies.

\- Study competing website-building platforms.

\- Collect feedback about pricing expectations.

\- Identify the most requested website types.



\### Questions to Ask Potential Customers



1\. Do you currently have a website for your business or services?

2\. How did you create your current website?

3\. What difficulties did you experience during website development?

4\. How much would you be willing to pay for a professional website?

5\. Would you prefer customizing a ready-made template or hiring a developer?

6\. Which website features are most important to you?

7\. Would you use a visual editor to customize your own website?

8\. What would make you choose Webify over other platforms?



\### Validation Success Criteria



\- Complete at least 15 customer interviews across the target segments.

\- Identify the three most requested website categories.

\- Understand customer budget expectations.

\- Identify common problems with existing website solutions.

\- Obtain feedback on the proposed Webify concept.

\- Use the findings to prioritize MVP features.



\---



\## 9. Out-of-Scope Customers for the Initial MVP



The initial version of Webify will not specifically target:



\- Large enterprise organizations requiring complex internal systems.

\- Businesses requiring highly specialized software platforms.

\- Customers requiring extensive custom backend integrations.

\- Organizations requiring dedicated enterprise infrastructure.



These customers may be considered in future development phases.



\---



\## 10. Conclusion



Webify will initially focus on small businesses, freelancers, and entrepreneurs who need accessible and affordable professional websites.



Digital agencies will represent an additional customer segment with potential for future growth through advanced features and agency-oriented services.



By understanding these customer groups and validating their needs, Webify can develop a product that solves real problems rather than building features without market demand.
